Digital resume

Technologist. Builder. Systems thinker.

I am Bill Whalen, a Minneapolis-based technologist at Microsoft. I work at the seam between people, AI, and business systems: finding a stubborn workflow, framing the real outcome, and making the idea tangible enough to test.

The artifact might be a multi-agent team, a Dynamics 365 accelerator, a voice-first interface, an operating system for field work, or a game. The common thread is practical: working software, clear boundaries, and an adoption path.

How I work

Working principles

  1. 01

    Start with the outcome.

    Define what changes for the person doing the work before choosing the model or platform.

  2. 02

    Build the proof.

    A working prototype creates better decisions than another round of abstract architecture.

  3. 03

    Keep humans at the consequential gates.

    Automation should increase leverage without hiding uncertainty, ownership, or irreversible action.

  4. 04

    Package what survives.

    Turn validated patterns into accelerators, skills, and handoffs other builders can use responsibly.

A form takes five seconds to open. Where do we look first?

  1. 1. What you bringFictional Live monitor observation: FullLoad is 5000 ms; customScriptTime is 3000 ms within the same load.
  2. 2. Help to reviewThe total is 5000 ms, not 8000 ms. Script time is a contributor inside that total, not an extra delay to add.
  3. 3. Your next decisionReview the matching request and script attribution. These two timings do not prove a script defect or identify the remaining time.

Someone suggests disabling plug-ins to make the app faster. What must happen first?

  1. 1. What you bringFictional proposal: disable every synchronous plug-in. No measured baseline, dependency review or rollback plan is available.

Help for the work around the issue.

Eight guided workflows connect the investigation. They prepare analysis and plans; they do not execute changes.

Guided workflow

Scope a performance investigation

A concise incident card: impact, reproducible scenario, known facts, missing evidence, candidate diagnostic path and next owner.

Guided workflow

Explain a slow form or page

A sourced timing breakdown or table, suspected contributor with confidence, contradictory evidence and a minimal next test.

Guided workflow

Review plug-in and server-side evidence

A correlation table with known durations, exclusions and uncertainties, plus a developer handoff or bounded capture proposal.

Guided workflow

Review integration load and throttling

A load/error/retry evidence table, prioritized hypotheses and a guarded test plan. No guarantee of throughput or license entitlement.

Guided workflow

Review environment health and capacity

An environment health brief: sourced trends, incident/change timeline, unknowns and next observation or support escalation.

Guided workflow

Review solution and customization risks

A finding-to-symptom matrix with sources, confidence, owner, proposed test and unresolved coverage.

Guided workflow

Plan and verify a controlled improvement

A review-only change card and before/after verification checklist with rollback, owner and explicit pending approvals.

Guided workflow

Turn diagnostics into an administrator review

A short administrator brief with sourced table/optional supported chart, hypothesis confidence, next decision and the actual no-change/changed/verified state.
